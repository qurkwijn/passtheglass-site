Your wine box in Pass the Glass

Do you sell wine boxes to taste at home? Put a QR code on the card in the box. Your customer scans it in Pass the Glass and every wine is ready for the tasting, with its name, vintage, price and a photo of the label.

You bring the wines, we take care of the tech. Works from version 1.5 of the app.

How it works for your customer

  1. New tasting. Your customer opens Pass the Glass and taps Scan Wine Box under the wines.
  2. Scan. The app reads the QR code on the card and fetches the wines.
  3. Taste. The wines are ready and the tasting has your box's name. Add the tasters, and pass the phone around.

The app is free for your customer. The results stay on their phone: the app sends nothing back and nothing is tracked. Want to know which wine won? Ask on the card, for example by WhatsApp or email.

Try it yourself

Scan this QR code in Pass the Glass, under New Tasting › Scan Wine Box. You'll get an example box with four made-up wines, with photos of the labels.

What we do for you

  1. You send the wine list. An email or a spreadsheet is enough: the wines, vintages and prices, and if you like, photos of the labels and a food pairing tip.
  2. We make the box. We put your wines into the app's format, with your shop's name and the box's name, and put it online at a fixed address.
  3. You get the QR code. Ready to print, with a short text for the card. We test the box in the app before you print.
  4. Changes are always possible. A typo, or a different wine in the box? We update it, and the QR code on your cards keeps working.

What it costs

That depends on how many boxes you make and how often they change. Email us what you have in mind, and we'll make a proposal that fits your shop.
