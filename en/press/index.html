Facts

AppPass the Glass: taste wine together. One phone goes around the table, everyone gives their own score, and at the end comes the reveal with the winner and the Best Value.
PriceFree, with no account, ads or tracking
Runs onAny iPhone with iOS 27 and any iPad with iPadOS 27, designed for iPhone Duo (tabletop, book, open and closed)
Wine boxScan the QR code on the card with your iPhone or iPad camera: the app opens with every wine ready. Without an iPhone or iPad, you taste a wine box in your browser. For wine shops, Qurk Wijn handles the tech: how it works
LanguagesEnglish and Dutch
Age18+, drink responsibly
